## Overview

As interest in AI grew across Fathom in 2023–24, an internal AI team began exploring emerging tools and establishing guidelines for responsible use, particularly around client information and data security.

With those guidelines in place, I began exploring how AI could improve our content development process. One opportunity stood out: turning the wealth of information captured across client engagements into compelling case studies.

In 2024–25, I independently developed a custom GPT to help transform existing client presentation materials into initial case study content, reducing the time from source material to a first draft from approximately one to three weeks to just a few hours.

## The Challenge

Fathom had meaningful client work and plenty of information to tell its story. The challenge was turning that information into compelling, accurate case studies.

Quarterly business reviews (QBRs) and conference presentations contained detailed client challenges, strategies, and outcomes. But these materials were created for different audiences and purposes, so identifying and shaping an external-facing story required significant review and interpretation.

The process also depended on available time and input from busy client teams. We needed a way to tell more stories, more efficiently.

## The Approach

Rather than introduce an entirely new content-gathering process, I looked at the information we already had.

QBR presentations were a natural starting point. They contained valuable context and details, but needed to be interpreted and shaped into a different kind of narrative.

I began experimenting with ChatGPT, using existing client-approved or appropriately white-labeled presentation materials to explore how AI could identify and organize relevant information.

Through repeated testing, prompting, and refinement, I independently developed a custom GPT designed around the case study development process and the content most valuable for marketing and sales enablement.

I built a specific instruction framework and refined its reference materials based on real-world use, improving the relevance, consistency, and accuracy of its output.

The goal wasn’t simply to generate copy. It was to create a reliable starting point for a more efficient content development process, making better use of information already available across the organization.

> Just as importantly, I recognized that AI couldn’t replace the judgment required to tell a good story.

Presentations sometimes contained multiple potential stories, and the context behind the information mattered. Reviewing the output, identifying inaccuracies, and validating details with subject-matter experts remained essential.

## The Solution

The Case Study GPT became a purpose-built resource for transforming complex presentation materials into structured case study drafts.

Instead of starting with a blank page, I could use existing source materials to identify the challenge, approach, and outcomes, then focus my time on refining the narrative, validating details, and developing content for its intended audience.

The tool supported the creative process rather than replacing it, helping reduce the administrative effort involved in getting a story started while keeping editorial judgment and accuracy at the center.

## Impact

<Stat before="1–3 weeks" after="A few hours" label="Time from QBR materials to initial case study content" />

The Case Study GPT reduced the time needed to move from QBR materials to initial case study content from approximately one to three weeks to just a few hours.

Beyond the time savings, the success of the initial GPT demonstrated how purpose-built AI tools could support other parts of the content development process.

I went on to build two additional custom GPTs:

- **Brand Voice GPT:** Supported consistent application of Fathom’s brand voice across content. This framework also helped support the internal brand transition process as Fathom prepared to sunset its identity.
- **Meta & Keyword GPT:** Helped streamline the development of metadata and keyword-related content.

Together, these tools created a small collection of AI resources supporting different aspects of content creation.

After presenting the Case Study GPT to leadership and receiving approval, I introduced it at a company-wide all-hands meeting and made it available to the sales team.

The Case Study GPT continues to be part of my workflow today.

## Reflection

AI is evolving quickly, and the tools available today will continue to change. That doesn’t change my approach: the value isn’t in adopting every new tool, but in understanding where technology can make a process better.

I’ll continue to evaluate and refine how I use AI, with a focus on keeping workflows relevant, outputs accurate, and content timely.

Technology may change, but the responsibility to deliver thoughtful, reliable work doesn’t.
