With the YMCA of the USA updating their brand, they needed a new membership campaign. A total of three concepts were produced, each consisting of a poster, a direct-mail postcard, and an ad that could be placed in local newspapers.

Working with [Keith Berr Productions](https://www.keithberr.com/), the campaign resulted in a five-day photo shoot and over 10,000 images. I led, directed, and organized over 20 different models.
