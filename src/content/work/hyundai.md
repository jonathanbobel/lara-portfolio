The Tire Guide was a lengthy project targeting Service Consultants, giving them a breakdown of what their tire program offered. It was sent out nationwide to over 500 consultants, and the content was Hyundai-specific.

The final piece was wire-bound, with laminated tabs for each section. I organized all photo shoots and retouching with our in-house photographer. After it went out, it was such a successful document that Hyundai asked us to create a PDF version to have digitally available.
