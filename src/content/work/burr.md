The target audience of this project was tour groups. The client wanted a fun print and digital campaign that would bring new business to the area. Following brand guidelines wasn't necessary, so using my creativity, I thought something with a Monty Python feel would do the trick.

The campaign consisted of a direct-mail piece, a newspaper ad, and an interactive video. I was able to lead and direct our in-house videographer.
