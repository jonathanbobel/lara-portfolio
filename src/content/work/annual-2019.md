The 2019 report resulted in a 25-page, black-and-white layout with highlights of DT blue that incorporated abstract images from throughout DT HQ. It focused on our 2019 goals and how each goal played a part in one of four Core Values.

The copywriter, photographer, and I collaborated extensively to make sure the final product was a success. We received high praise from department leadership.
