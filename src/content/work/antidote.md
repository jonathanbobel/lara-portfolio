A new business needed a brand identity, website, and online presence. I interviewed the owner, listened to her vision of her ideal brand, and we coordinated the brand launch with her graduation. Business cards and social media assets came first, then the website followed.

I taught myself Sketch to present to the client, with the added benefit of assisting the web developer. The final product was a solid online presence that allowed her to expand her clientele and hire another associate to round out her business.
