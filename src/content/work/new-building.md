Working closely with HR and communications around our building move, we decided to create a Move-In Kit. It included a calendar, notebook, and parking pass. The calendar had information about moving out of our old building and into our new one; after the move, it became a fully functioning 2017 desk calendar.

GRIT is a term Dealer Tire uses to describe itself and its associates — we get down to business and do what's needed. Being able to bring GRIT to life in a small yet visible way was important. The parking pass, while small, was a fun and challenging opportunity to breathe life into Dealer Tire's brand.
