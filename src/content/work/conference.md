Create a B2B and a B2C booth for the Jaguar/Land Rover Annual Conference with a tire-centric message that was clear and concise. Both events took place in Coachella, CA, and the creative direction I took utilized the latitude/longitude coordinates.

I initiated design layouts for the B2C booth while waiting for approval on the B2B booth, and collaborated with the copywriter and stakeholders to make sure the messaging and design complemented each other and worked within JLR's brand.

The B2B booth was a success based on the number of visitors and email sign-ups. Because of my initiative with the B2C booth, we gained approval and Goodyear agreed to partner with us, providing the funding needed.
