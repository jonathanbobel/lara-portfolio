The UCC Insurance Board requested four messages that needed to be communicated to all their members. My solution was 11×17 posters that could easily be printed internally or at a local print shop.

The client wanted specific scenes, and with a very small budget, a photo shoot was out of the question. Instead, I created illustrations of those scenes, which gave me the freedom to change whatever I needed. The lack of specific facial features was intentional — I wanted the audience to be able to picture themselves or a loved one in the poster.
